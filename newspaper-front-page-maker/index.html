A newspaper generator with your photo

This newspaper generator makes a full front page in your browser. You pick a layout, add a photo and write the headline. Every word on the page can change: the newspaper name, the date, the price, the small boxes beside the name, the main story, the second story and the strip at the bottom. You then download the page as a PNG or a PDF, print it, or share it.

Your photo stays on your device. The page reads it with your browser and draws it on the page. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. The download has no watermark.

How to make a newspaper front page

  1. Pick the occasion. Birthday, anniversary, wedding, retirement, graduation, new baby or an office joke. Type the name, and the age or the years if it asks. The text fills in for you.
  2. Pick a look. Classic broadsheet, tabloid or vintage 1920s. Your text and photos stay when you switch.
  3. Add a photo. Tap Add photo or drop a picture on the page. Drag it on the page to move it, and use Zoom to crop it.
  4. Change the words. Tap any text on the page and type. Tap Surprise me for a new headline. The All text list has every part in one place.
  5. Download. Choose a size, then Download PNG or Download PDF. Copy link gives you a link with your words (not your photos), so a friend can open the page and add a picture.

Front page ideas by occasion

  • Birthday newspaper: "Sam Turns 40, Town Declares a Holiday." Print it for the party table, or post it in the family group chat.
  • Anniversary: a front page about how the couple met, with an old photo in the second story.
  • Wedding: "Alex and Jordan Tie the Knot." Print copies for the guest tables, or send one to family who could not come.
  • Retirement: "Pat Retires After 30 Great Years." Coworkers can sign the printed page like a card.
  • Graduation: a "Class of 2026" edition with a first-day-of-school photo next to the cap and gown.
  • New baby: a front page announcement to send to family, or to keep in the baby book.
  • Office joke: "Chris Fixes the Printer on the First Try." Keep it kind.
  • School project: a vintage front page for a history lesson or a book report. No student account is needed.

The parts of a newspaper front page

Students often need to label a front page. These are the parts this tool uses:

  • Nameplate (masthead): the name of the newspaper across the top.
  • Ears: the small boxes on each side of the name, often with the weather.
  • Dateline row: volume and issue number, the date and the price.
  • Kicker: a short word above the headline, such as "Extra".
  • Headline and deck: the big title and the smaller line under it.
  • Byline: who wrote the story.
  • Caption: the line under a photo.
  • Teasers: the strip that points to stories inside the paper.

Tips for a good headline

  • Use the present tense: "Sam Turns 40", not "Sam Turned 40".
  • Keep it short. Six to eight words fill the line at a big size.
  • Put the name first. People look for the name before anything else.
  • Add a small, true detail: the cake, the dog, the old car.

Keep it a novelty

A front page you make here is a gift or a joke among friends. Each page has a small "Novelty print. Fictional." line under the name. You can turn it off under Look. The PNG file keeps this note in its details either way. Please do not use the tool to fool people or to say false things about a real person. The layouts are our own designs and do not copy any real newspaper.

Frequently asked questions

Is this newspaper generator free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads. You can print the page or post it as often as you like.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser reads the photo and draws it on the page on your own device. The photo is not sent to a server and it is not saved. You can check this in the Network tab of your browser tools.

Can I change every word on the page?

Yes. Tap or click any text on the page to change it, including the newspaper name, the date, the price and the small boxes beside the name. The All text on the page list shows every part as a form field too.

What sizes can I download?

Letter and A4 for printing, as a PNG or a PDF at 300 dots per inch. For social media you get a 1080 x 1350 post, a 1080 x 1080 square and a 1080 x 1920 story. Each size has its own layout.

How do I print the newspaper so it fits the page?

Choose Letter or A4 under Size, then Download PDF. In the print dialog, choose Actual size or 100%. If your printer cannot print to the edge, choose White paper under Look to save ink.

Can I make a birthday newspaper with the person’s age?

Yes. Pick Birthday, then type the name and the age. The headline, the story and the captions use them. If you change the name later, it changes everywhere on the page. The tool does not add real news from a past date.

Can teachers use it for a class project?

Yes. Students do not need an account. The vintage layout suits history lessons and book reports, and the Letter PDF prints well for a hallway display.

Is it OK to make a fake newspaper front page?

It is fine as a gift or a joke among friends. Do not use it to mislead people or to say false things about a real person. Each page has a small Novelty print line that you can turn off, and the PNG keeps this note in its file details.
