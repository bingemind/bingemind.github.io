Questions, answered

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp or by email.

How do you handle data privacy?

We give each system only the access it needs. For sensitive work, we can run open-source models such as Llama on your own servers or private cloud, so your data stays under your control.

Which tools do you use?

We pick per project. Common choices are n8n for workflows, Whisper for speech to text, open-source or hosted language models, and LangChain or LlamaIndex for search over your documents. We connect to the tools you already use, such as your CRM, email and WhatsApp Business.

How long does a project take?

A simple assistant can go live in 3 to 5 business days. Bigger systems take longer. You get a timeline in the plan before we start.

Open source or custom: which do I need?

Usually both. Open source covers the common parts, such as speech, models and workflow engines. Custom code covers your rules, your systems and the edge cases. The audit shows you the split.

Do you work with clients outside India? Which time zones?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, remotely. We set call times that suit your time zone, and most of the work happens over email, WhatsApp and shared documents.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Ask on WhatsApp and we will show you a working demo that fits your use case.

How do we start?

Book a free audit on WhatsApp or by email. We have a short call, then send you a plan. You decide if you want to go ahead.
