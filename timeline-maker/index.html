    Edit events

    What is a timeline maker?

    A timeline shows events in the order they happened, on one line. A timeline maker draws that line for you, places each event, and keeps the spacing neat when you add or remove one. This timeline maker works in your browser. You can add photos, pick a size from a letter page to an 11 x 17 poster, and download the result.

    How to use the timeline maker

    1. Type a title, or pick an example to start from.
    2. Add your events. Each one needs a date and a short title. The month, the day and a note are optional.
    3. Add a photo to any event if you want one.
    4. Choose Across or Down, a size and a color.
    5. Check the preview. Shorten notes if the page says some text was shortened.
    6. Download a PNG or a PDF, or print the poster.

    How many events should a timeline have?

    For a printed page, 6 to 12 events read well. A poster can hold up to 24. If you have more, split the story into two timelines, such as one per part of a project or one per decade. Short titles of three to six words are easier to read from across a room.

    Across or down?

    • Across puts the line from left to right. It suits landscape pages, slides and wall posters.
    • Down puts the line from top to bottom. It suits portrait pages, phone screens and posts.

    Events take turns on each side of the line, so the cards have more room.

    Dates, labels and order

    Each event can have a year, a month and year, or a full date. The tool sorts events by date. When two events have the same date, they keep the order you added them in. If a timeline is not about dates, such as the plot of a book, use a label instead, for example "Chapter 3". Then you set the order yourself with Move up and Move down.

    Timeline examples

    • History or science project: the main steps of a topic, each with a picture you took or drew.
    • Project plan: kickoff, research, first draft, review, launch, with real dates.
    • Family story: the years that matter to your family, with old photos, for an anniversary or a reunion.
    • Book plot: the key scenes of a story, by chapter, to plan or to study a book.
    • School year: the big days from the first day to the last.

    Photos stay on your device

    When you add a photo, your browser reads it and draws it on the timeline. The photo is not uploaded and it is not saved. If you reload the page, your text comes back but the photos do not, so add them again before you download. Photos are cropped to fit their card. Use the photo position setting to keep the top, the middle or the bottom of the picture.

    Printing a timeline poster

    Choose a print size and download the PDF. Print it at 100 percent or "Actual size". The 11 x 17 inch size is a common poster size that many print shops and some home printers handle. For a bigger wall poster, download the PNG and use the Poster tiler to split it over several letter or A4 sheets, then tape them together.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this timeline maker free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no download limit.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Your browser reads each photo and draws it on your device. Photos are not sent to a server and they are not saved. You can check this in the Network tab of your browser tools.

    How many events can I add?

    Up to 24 events and up to 12 photos on one timeline. For more, make two timelines.

    What sizes can I download?

    Letter and A4, landscape or portrait, an 11 x 17 inch poster, a 16:9 slide and a 4:5 post. Print sizes come as a PNG or a PDF.

    Can I print a big timeline poster?

    Yes. The 11 x 17 inch size is a poster. For a bigger poster, download the PNG and split it over several sheets with the Poster tiler.

    Can I use dates before year 1?

    Not yet. Years from 1 to 9999 work. For older history, use a label such as "500 BC" and set the order yourself.

    Can students use it for school?

    Yes. Students do not need an account. The Letter and 11 x 17 sizes print well for class projects.

    Is my work saved?

    Your text and settings are saved in this browser on this device, so a reload does not lose them. Photos are not saved. Use Start over to clear everything.
