    How words and characters are counted

    • Words are runs of letters or numbers. "Don't", "e-mail" and "2026" each count as one word. For languages written without spaces, such as Chinese, Japanese and Thai, the counter uses your browser's built-in language rules to find word boundaries.
    • Characters include letters, numbers, spaces, punctuation and line breaks. Emoji count as one character each.
    • Sentences end with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark (including the Chinese and Japanese forms).
    • Paragraphs are blocks of text separated by a blank line.
    • Reading time uses 238 words a minute, the average silent reading speed for adults reading English non-fiction. Speaking time uses 150 words a minute, a comfortable presentation pace. For Chinese and Japanese, times are based on characters.
    • Keyword density = times a word appears ÷ total words × 100. Common words such as "the" and "and" are left out.

    How many pages is that?

    At 12-point font with single spacing, a page holds about 500 words. Double-spaced, about 250. So 1,000 words is roughly 2 single-spaced or 4 double-spaced pages.

    Writing within a limit

    • Cut filler words: "very", "really", "just", "actually", "in order to" (use "to"), "due to the fact that" (use "because").
    • Prefer active verbs: "We decided" is shorter and clearer than "A decision was made by us".
    • One idea per sentence. If the average sentence length shown above is over 25 words, splitting a few long sentences helps readers.
    • Watch repeated keywords. In web writing, a keyword density above about 2% to 3% can read as repetitive. Write for people first.
    • Reading ease: a Flesch score of 60 to 70 suits most audiences. Shorter words and sentences raise it.

    Characters versus words

    Social networks, SMS and SEO tags limit characters, because they display text in a fixed space. Schools, publishers and application forms usually limit words. Some, such as UCAS, count characters including spaces, so check which one applies.

    Privacy

    Your text is counted inside your browser. It is not uploaded, logged or sent anywhere. The last text you typed is kept in this browser's local storage so it is still there if you reload the page; press Clear to remove it.

    Word count questions

    How do I count words in my text?

    Paste or type the text into the box. The word count updates instantly, along with characters, sentences, paragraphs and reading time.

    Do spaces count as characters?

    Yes, in most limits, including X posts, SMS and UCAS statements. The counter shows characters both with and without spaces.

    How long does it take to read 1,000 words?

    About 4 minutes at an average silent reading speed of 238 words a minute, and about 7 minutes to say out loud at 150 words a minute.

    How many words is a 5-minute speech?

    About 650 to 750 words at a natural speaking pace of 130 to 150 words a minute.

    Does the word counter work for Chinese and Japanese?

    Yes. It uses your browser’s language-aware word segmentation, so text without spaces between words is counted properly.

    Are hyphenated words counted as one word?

    Yes. "Well-known" and "e-mail" count as one word each, which matches how Microsoft Word and Google Docs count.

    Is my text stored or uploaded?

    No. Counting happens in your browser. The text is only kept in your own browser’s storage until you clear it.
