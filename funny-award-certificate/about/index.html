About

Funny Award Certificate Maker is a free tool that makes funny award certificates. Pick an award, type a name and print it, or save it as a PDF or PNG. You can also paste a list of names and get one PDF with a page for each person.

Who it is for

Office teams, teachers, coaches, parents and friends. Anyone who wants to give a small, kind award at a party, at the end of a season or at the end of the school year.

How to use it

Choose who the awards are for, then pick an idea from the list or press Random award. Type the name. Choose a design, a color and the paper size. Then press Download PDF, PNG or Print. For a whole class or team, switch to Many names and paste the list.

How it works

The certificate is drawn in your browser. The designs, seals, ribbons and trophies are our own drawings. The PDF is made on your device too, so the names you type are not sent to a server. We do not ask you to create an account, and there is no watermark. The tool saves your design and text choices on your own device. It does not save names.
