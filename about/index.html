About Bingemind

Free browser tools for small jobs that should take less time.

What you can do here

Bingemind makes free online tools for files, printing, calculations and everyday tasks. You can turn photos into a PDF, make a newspaper front page, print a banner or scale a cake recipe. Other tools help with gym weights, knitting, building measurements and lists of text. The tools hub groups them by the job you want to do.

You do not need to create an account or sign up to use the tools. Open a page, enter what you need and use the result. Many tools let you copy an answer, save a file or print a sheet. Each page explains the options and gives examples, so you can check whether it fits your task before you start.

Who makes Bingemind

Bingemind is made by Abhishek Mishra, a software engineer and co-founder based in Lucknow, India. He builds and maintains the tools. Bingemind also offers automation services for businesses. Those services have their own page so you can browse the free tools without booking a call.

How tools are chosen and checked

We choose small, specific tasks where a browser tool can give a useful result without a long setup. A tool should have a clear purpose, explain its inputs and make its output easy to use. We check calculations with example values and check file tools with sample files. We also check page layouts on phone and desktop sizes, including light and dark themes.

These checks cannot catch every problem. A result may depend on the values you enter, your browser or a file format. Check important calculations yourself. Use the guidance on each page and keep a copy of any file you need.

Your input stays with you

The tools process your text, pictures and files in your browser. We do not upload that input to our servers. Some tools save settings on your device. Analytics and advertising are separate from tool input. The privacy policy explains those services and your choices.

Help us improve a tool

If something goes wrong, contact us with the page address, what you tried and what happened. Please leave out private files or personal details. We also welcome tool ideas and suggestions that make an existing tool easier to use.
