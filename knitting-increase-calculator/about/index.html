About

Knitting Increase Calculator spreads increases or decreases evenly across a knitted or crocheted row. Choose knit or crochet. You type the stitches you have and the number to add or remove. It writes the pattern line and draws your row.

What it does

Patterns say "increase 12 stitches evenly" and leave the sums to you. This tool does the sums and gives a line you can copy.

How it works

The tool runs in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account. It handles flat rows and work in the round, increases and decreases, and the common stitch names such as M1, YO, KFB, K2tog and SSK for knitting, and sc, inc and sc2tog for crochet.
