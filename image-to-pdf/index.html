    How to convert an image into a PDF

    This image to PDF converter turns photos, scans and screenshots into a PDF in your browser. It works as a JPG to PDF, PNG to PDF and HEIC to PDF converter, and also takes WebP, GIF and BMP files. Nothing is uploaded, so it is safe for an ID, a pay stub or a medical form.

    1. Press Choose images, or drop the files on the box. On a phone you can pick from your photo library or take a new photo.
    2. Check the order. Each card is one page. Drag a card, or use its arrow buttons. The rotate button turns a page a quarter turn.
    3. Pick the page size, margin and quality on the right. The cards change at once, so you see each page before you download.
    4. Press Download PDF. The file is saved like any other download.

    Paste an image into a PDF

    You do not need to save a screenshot first. Copy an image, or take a screenshot to the clipboard, then press Ctrl+V on this page (Cmd+V on a Mac). The image is added as the next page. Some browsers also show a Paste button. Paste as many times as you like, then download one PDF with all the screenshots. This is handy for bug reports, receipts from a web page or notes for a class.

    On Windows, Win+Shift+S copies part of the screen. On a Mac, Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4 does the same.

    Insert an image into an existing PDF

    Add your PDF together with the images. Every page of the PDF appears as its own card, marked "PDF". Put the images where you want them: before the first page, after the last page, or between two pages. Then download. The original PDF pages keep their size and content. The new pages use the page size you chose. A PDF with a password cannot be read until you unlock it.

    How to turn an image into a PDF on iPhone

    With this tool: open this page in Safari, tap Choose images, then Photo Library. Select your photos and tap Add. Tap Download PDF. Then tap Share to save the PDF to Files or send it by Mail or Messages. HEIC photos work, and the location data in them is removed.

    Without a website, there are two built-in ways:

    • Files app: save the photos to Files, select them, then choose Create PDF. It is under the More button or under Quick Actions, depending on your iOS version. You cannot set the page size or margins.
    • Print trick: in Photos, select the photos, tap Share, then Print. Spread two fingers on the preview to open it as a PDF, then share it. The pages are always printer size.

    Auto orientation gives a wide photo a landscape page and a tall photo a portrait page, so every image is as large as it can be. Whole image shows all of the picture. Fill page covers the page and cuts off the edges that do not fit. Letter is 8.5 by 11 inches and is the US standard. A4 is 210 by 297 mm and is used in most other countries.

    Size depends on the photo. As a rough guide, a 12 megapixel phone photo makes a page of about 2 to 4 MB at Original, about 1 MB at High, under 0.5 MB at Medium and about 0.2 MB at Small. The tool shows the real size of your PDF after you download it.

    Why your photos stay on your device

    Most image to PDF sites send your files to their servers, make the PDF there and send it back. This page does the work in your browser with JavaScript. Your images are never sent anywhere, there is no account, and no copy is kept. When you close the tab, the files are gone from the page.

    The tool also removes the hidden data that phones add to photos, such as the place where you took them. The PDF holds only the pictures you see.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. The PDF is made in your browser, on your device. The page does not send your images or PDFs anywhere. You can check this: open the Network tab in your browser tools, add a few images and download the PDF. You will see no upload.

    Is it free? Are there limits?

    It is free, with no sign-up and no watermark. There is no limit on the number of images or the file size. The only limit is the memory of your device. On a phone, 50 or more full-size photos can be slow. Choose High or Medium quality for big batches.

    Can I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

    Yes. Pick them from the photo library on your iPhone, or drop .heic files on a computer. Safari opens HEIC directly. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox the tool loads a HEIC reader the first time it needs one and converts the photo on your device.

    How do I make the PDF smaller?

    Choose Medium or Small quality. Medium makes photos at most 2000 pixels on the long side, which is enough for email and upload forms. Small goes to 1400 pixels. Original puts JPG and PNG files in unchanged, so the PDF is about as big as those files. Other formats, such as HEIC and WebP, become high quality JPG.

    Can I put several images in one PDF?

    Yes. Add as many images as you need. Each image becomes one page, in the order you see. Drag a page or use the arrow buttons to change the order, or use Sort by name. To get a separate PDF for each image, choose One per image under Output. You then get one ZIP file.

    Can I add an image to a PDF I already have?

    Yes. Add the PDF and the images together. Each page of the PDF shows as its own card. Move the images before, after or between those pages, then download. The PDF pages keep their size. A PDF that is locked with a password must be unlocked first.

    Does the PDF keep the location data of my photos?

    No. Phone photos often store the place and time they were taken in EXIF data. The tool removes that data from every photo before it goes into the PDF.

    Why is my photo sideways?

    Phones save many photos sideways and add a note that says how to turn them. The tool reads that note and turns the page for you. If a photo still looks wrong, press the rotate button on its card. The preview shows the page exactly as it will be in the PDF.
