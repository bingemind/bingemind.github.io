    What are open when letters?

    Open when letters are a set of sealed letters, each one marked with a moment, like "Open when you miss me" or "Open when you need a laugh". The person keeps them and opens each one when that moment comes. They make a thoughtful gift for a partner far away, a friend, a student leaving home or a new graduate.

    This open when letters kit gives you the ideas, the envelope labels and a writing sheet for each letter. You only need envelopes, glue or tape, and something to write with.

    How to make open when letters

    1. Tick the ideas you want, or type your own.
    2. Choose the size of your envelopes.
    3. Pick a style, and add the name of the person the letters are for.
    4. Download the PDF or press Print. Print at 100% or "Actual size".
    5. Cut out the labels and glue or tape one on each envelope.
    6. Write each letter on its sheet, fold it as shown and seal it in the matching envelope.

    Open when letter ideas

    The kit has 36 ideas in five groups. Anytime ideas fit anyone: when you miss me, when you need a laugh, when you cannot sleep, when you had a bad day. Partner ideas include when we just had a fight and when it is our anniversary. Leaving home ideas cover the first day, missing home and the first time doing laundry. Graduate ideas cover the first job and a first home. Add your own ideas too: the best ones are about moments only the two of you know.

    What to write in an open when letter

    Each letter sheet has a short tip. Most good letters have three parts: why you are writing, one real memory or detail, and something to do now, like a song to play or a plan for the next visit. Keep each letter to one page. Add small things to the envelope if they fit, such as a photo, a tea bag or a sticker.

    Envelope sizes and folding

    Choose the envelopes you have. The labels are sized to fit them:

    • A2 (4 3/8 x 5 3/4 in): labels 3.5 x 2 in. Fold a Letter sheet in half, then in half again.
    • A7 (5 1/4 x 7 1/4 in): labels 3.5 x 2.5 in. Fold a Letter or A4 sheet in half, then in half again.
    • #10 business (4 1/8 x 9 1/2 in): labels 3.5 x 1.75 in. Fold a Letter sheet in thirds.
    • C6 (114 x 162 mm): labels 85 x 50 mm. Fold an A4 sheet in half, then in half again.
    • DL (110 x 220 mm): labels 85 x 45 mm. Fold an A4 sheet in thirds.

    Print the labels on plain paper and glue them on, or use full-page sticker paper and cut them out.

    How many letters to make

    Ten to twelve letters is a good set for a gift. For a long distance relationship or a first year away, 20 or more lets the person open one every few weeks. Number the envelopes if you want them opened in order.
