Make a baby announcement card in minutes

This baby announcement maker makes a birth card from one photo and a few details. Type the name, the date, the time, the weight and the length. Pick a layout and colors, then print the card or save it for your phone. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Your photo stays on your device. The page reads it in your browser and draws the card there. Nothing is uploaded and the photo is not saved. When you close the tab, it is gone.

How to make a baby announcement

  1. Choose the kind of card: Birth, Welcome home or Coming soon.
  2. Add a photo. Drag it to move it and use the slider to zoom.
  3. Type the name, the date and the time. Add the weight and length, in the units you use.
  4. Pick a layout and colors. Change any words you like.
  5. Pick a size. Use 5 x 7 or 4 x 6 to print, or post, square or story for your phone.
  6. Download a PNG or a PDF, or press Print. Print at 100% and cut on the marks.

What to write on a baby announcement

Most cards have the same parts: the baby's full name, the date of birth, the time, the weight and the length. Many add the parents' names and one short line, such as "Welcome to the world, little one". Some add the place of birth. Keep it short. People read the name and the photo first. Every line on the card can change, and you can switch off any detail you do not want to share.

Weight and length in your units

Type the weight in pounds and ounces or in kilograms, and the length in inches or centimeters. The card shows the units you choose. Turn on "Show both units" if family lives in other countries. For example, 7 lb 8 oz is 3.40 kg, and 20 inches is 50.8 cm.

Sizes for print and for phones

The 5 x 7 inch card is the most common size for mailed announcements. 4 x 6 inch fits a standard photo print. Both download at 300 dots per inch. Choose a Letter or A4 sheet to print at home: the card sits in the middle with cut marks. Choose "Card size" to send the file to a photo shop. For phones, use the post, square or story size.

Pregnancy and adoption announcements

Choose Coming soon to announce a pregnancy. The date becomes the due month, and the words change to match. Choose Welcome home for an adoption or a foster arrival. The words talk about the day the child came home. You can edit every word in both.

Four layouts

Classic puts the photo on top and the name below. Split puts the photo on one side and the details on the other. Frame uses the whole card for the photo with a white panel for the words. Front page turns the news into a small newspaper-style notice, a fun choice for grandparents.

Your photo stays private

Baby photos are personal. This tool never sends your photo or your words to a server. The text you type is saved on this device so a reload does not lose it. The photo is not saved. Press Start over to clear the saved text.

Frequently asked questions

Is this baby announcement maker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads.

Is my baby's photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser reads the photo and draws the card on your own device. The photo is not sent to a server and it is not saved.

What size is a baby announcement card?

5 x 7 inches is the most common size for mailed cards. 4 x 6 inches fits a standard photo print. Both download at 300 dots per inch.

How do I print the card at the right size?

Download the PDF on a Letter or A4 sheet and print at 100% or "Actual size". Then cut on the marks. For a photo shop, choose Card size and send the PNG or PDF.

Can I use kilograms and centimeters?

Yes. Choose kg and cm, or turn on "Show both units" to show pounds, ounces and inches too.

Can I make a pregnancy announcement?

Yes. Choose Coming soon. The date becomes the due month and the words change to match.

What should I write on a birth announcement?

The full name, the date and time of birth, the weight and length, and the parents' names. Add one short line if you like, such as "Welcome to the world, little one".
