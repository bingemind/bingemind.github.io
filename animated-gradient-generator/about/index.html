About

Animated Gradient Background Generator is a free tool that makes animated gradient backgrounds. You pick colors, a type, the speed, blur and grain. Then you copy the CSS, or download a PNG or a short video loop.

Who it is for

Web builders and developers who want a moving background in plain CSS, designers who need a grainy gradient still, and creators who need a short loop for a video or a stream. It works on a phone or a computer.

How it works

The preview is real CSS in your browser. The PNG and the video are drawn on your device with the same math. Nothing is uploaded and you do not need an account. Video is offered only when your browser can record it.
