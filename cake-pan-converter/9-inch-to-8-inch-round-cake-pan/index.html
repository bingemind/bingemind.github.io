      Questions about this swap

      Can I bake a 9 inch cake recipe in an 8 inch pan?

      Yes, with 79% of the batter. The rest bakes as cupcakes. A full recipe in an 8 inch pan can spill over the rim.

      What fraction of the recipe is that?

      Close to 3/4. The exact factor is 0.79, and the tool rounds each amount to a size you can measure.
