      Questions about this swap

      Can I double a loaf recipe for a 9x13 pan?

      Almost. The exact factor is 1.89, so doubling gives about 6% too much batter. Use 1.89, or double it and bake a few muffins.

      Does a 9x13 bake faster than a loaf pan?

      Yes. The batter is shallower. Check at 70% to 90% of the loaf time at the same temperature.
