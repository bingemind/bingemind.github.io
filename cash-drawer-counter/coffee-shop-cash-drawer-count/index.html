      Closing a cafe drawer
           
      1. Print the end-of-shift report after the last sale.
      2. Take the tray out and count the cash once.
      3. Enter the cash sales from the report and any paid-outs. The calculator shows over or short.
      4. Leave the float in the drawer. Bag the deposit with the printed count.
      5. Sign the count. Have the manager sign it if your cafe requires.
        
      Why cafes run low on small bills
       
      Most drinks cost under $10, and many people pay with a $20 bill. Each one takes a $5 and several $1 bills from the drawer. By late morning the ones run low. If this happens often, raise the number of $1 bills in the float, or drop large bills to the safe more often.
            
      A morning check
       
      Count the drawer before the doors open. If your manager sets the float from the opening count, press "Use this count as my float". A short opening drawer is much easier to explain at 7 a.m. than at 3 p.m.
            
      Common questions
       
      How much should a coffee shop leave in the drawer?
      Many cafes use $100 to $200. A $150 float with lots of $1 and $5 bills works for a small shop. Your owner sets the number.
      Where do tips go?
      Keep tips out of the drawer. Count them apart and share them by your shop rule.
