        How to use the SOP maker

        1. Type the title. Add an SOP number, a version and the dates if your team uses them.
        2. Write the purpose in one or two sentences: why this procedure exists.
        3. Add the roles, the materials and any safety warnings, one per line.
        4. Add each step. Say who does it, and add a check if the person needs to confirm something.
        5. Use Move up and Move down to put the steps in order.
        6. Read the checks, then press Download PDF or Copy as Markdown.

        The preview updates as you type. Your SOP is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to it.

        What to include in an SOP

        A standard operating procedure helps people do a task the same way every time. A good SOP template has these parts:

        • Header facts. SOP number, version, effective date, next review date, area, owner and approver.
        • Purpose. Why the procedure exists, in one or two sentences.
        • Scope. Where it applies, and where it does not.
        • Roles and responsibilities. Who does what.
        • Materials and tools. What to have ready before you start.
        • Safety and warnings. What can hurt a person or damage equipment.
        • Steps. Numbered, one action each, with who does it and how to check it.
        • Revision history. What changed, when and by whom.
        • Sign-off. Lines for the people who prepare, review and approve it.

        How to write SOP steps

        Start each step with a verb: unlock, count, turn on, wipe. Keep one action per step. If a step needs the word "and" twice, split it. Write the step for the newest person on the team, not for the expert. The tool warns you when a step has more than 40 words.

        Add a check when the person needs to confirm a result before they go on. For example, "Count the till float and write the amount in the cash log", with the check "Float matches the log". The check prints under the step, next to a box to tick.

        Print it or keep it as text

        The PDF is made for paper. Each step has a box to tick, the tables repeat their header row on a new page, and the sign-off table has room for a signature. Choose US Letter or A4, and switch page numbers on or off.

        If your team keeps procedures in a wiki or a shared document, press Copy as Markdown. The steps come out as a table, so they keep their numbers and roles.

        Keep your SOP current

        An SOP is only useful if it matches the work. Set a next review date, and check the steps on the floor with the people who do the task. When something changes, raise the version, add a row to the revision history and print a new copy. Remove old printed copies, so nobody follows the wrong steps.

        Frequently asked questions

        What is an SOP?

        An SOP, or standard operating procedure, is a written set of steps for a task that people do the same way every time. It says who does each step, what they need, and how they know the step is done.

        What should an SOP include?

        A title, an SOP number and version, the purpose, the scope, the roles, the materials, any safety warnings, the numbered steps, a revision history, and sign-off lines. Only the title, the purpose and the steps are required in this tool.

        Does this tool write the SOP for me?

        No. You write every step. The tool lays your text out as a clean document, numbers the steps, adds a checkbox for each one and checks for missing parts.

        How do I save my SOP as a PDF?

        Press Download PDF. Your browser opens its print window. Choose Save as PDF. The SOP number and title become the file name. Page numbers are on unless you switch them off.

        Can I edit the SOP in a word processor?

        Press Copy as Markdown and paste it into any editor that reads Markdown, or into a wiki. You can also print the PDF and fill in the checkboxes and sign-off lines by hand.

        How often should an SOP be reviewed?

        Many teams review an SOP once or twice a year, and any time the task changes. Set the next review date in the tool, and add a row to the revision history when you change it.

        Is my SOP saved or sent anywhere?

        Your SOP is saved only in this browser, so it is still there when you come back. Nothing you type is sent to us. Press Clear all to remove it.
