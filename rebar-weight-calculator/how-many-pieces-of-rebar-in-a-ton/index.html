How to work it out

Bars in a ton equals the weight of a ton divided by the weight of one bar. The weight of one bar is the pounds per foot times its length.

Example: a 40 ft #6 bar weighs 40 × 1.502, or 60.08 lb. A short ton holds 2,000 / 60.08, or 33.3 of them. Round down to whole bars when you load a truck, and expect a few percent of difference from the mill weight.

Ton, short ton and tonne

In US trade, a ton is the short ton of 2,000 lb. A metric tonne is 1,000 kg, or 2,204.6 lb. Many quotes in the US also use CWT, which is 100 lb. Check the unit on each quote before you compare prices.
