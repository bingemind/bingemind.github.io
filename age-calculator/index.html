How age is calculated

Age is counted in completed calendar units, the way it is on passports and official forms.

  1. Count the whole years from the date of birth up to the last birthday.
  2. Count the whole months since that birthday.
  3. Count the days left over.

Example: born 15 June 1995, age on 15 November 2026. The last birthday was 15 June 2026, which makes 31 years. From 15 June to 15 November is 5 whole months, with 0 days left over. The age is 31 years, 5 months and 0 days.

Total days are counted exactly between the two dates, so leap days are included. Weeks, hours and minutes are derived from that total.

Leap years and month lengths

Months have 28 to 31 days, so "one month" is not a fixed length. The calculator always moves by calendar month: one month after 31 January is the last day of February.

Born on 29 February? In years that are not leap years, your birthday is treated as 1 March for the countdown, which is the legal rule in the UK and several other places. Some jurisdictions use 28 February instead.

A year is a leap year if it divides by 4, except century years, which must divide by 400. So 2000 and 2024 were leap years, 1900 was not, and 2100 will not be.

Different ways of counting age

  • International age: you are 0 at birth and gain a year on each birthday. This is what the calculator shows and what almost every country uses officially.
  • Korean traditional age: you are 1 at birth and everyone gains a year on 1 January. South Korea moved to international age for official purposes in June 2023, but the traditional count is still used socially. The result shows it for reference.
  • East Asian reckoning in parts of China and Vietnam works in a similar way to the Korean traditional count, tied to the lunar new year.
  • Age for school entry or sports is often fixed on a cut-off date, such as 1 September or 31 December. Put that date in the second field to check eligibility.

What people use an age calculator for

  • Forms and applications that ask for age in years and months, such as school admissions, visas and insurance.
  • Eligibility checks: minimum ages for driving, voting, pensions and competitions on a given date.
  • Babies and children: age in weeks and months for health checks and vaccination schedules.
  • Milestones: your 10,000th day comes at about 27 years and 4 months, and your billionth second at about 31 years and 8 months.
  • Time between any two dates: the calculator works for anniversaries, service length and project durations too.

Age questions

How do I calculate my exact age?

Count the full years since your date of birth, then the full months since your last birthday, then the remaining days. The calculator does this for any two dates.

How do I calculate age from date of birth in days?

Count every day between the date of birth and today, including leap days. The calculator shows this as "In days".

How old will I be on a future date?

Change the second date to the date you want. The age and next-birthday countdown update for that day.

How is age calculated for someone born on 29 February?

They gain a year on 29 February in leap years. In other years the birthday is treated as 1 March in many legal systems, including the UK, and as 28 February in some others.

What is Korean age?

In the traditional Korean system you are 1 at birth and add a year every 1 January. Since June 2023 South Korea uses international age for legal and official purposes.

Does the calculator include the day of birth?

It counts completed days, so the day of birth is day zero. A baby born yesterday is 1 day old today.
