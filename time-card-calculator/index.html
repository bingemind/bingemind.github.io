How to calculate hours on a time card

For each day, take the clock-out time, subtract the clock-in time, and subtract any unpaid break. Then add up the days. Payroll needs the total in decimal hours, so divide any leftover minutes by 60.

Daily hours = clock-out − clock-in − unpaid break
Decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)
Pay = regular hours × rate + overtime hours × rate × 1.5

Example

8:30 AM to 5:15 PM is 8 hours 45 minutes. Take off a 30-minute lunch and you get 8:15, which is 8.25 decimal hours. Five days like that make 41.25 hours: 40 regular and 1.25 overtime. At $20 an hour that is $800 + $37.50 = $837.50.

Time card questions

How do I calculate hours worked on a time card?

For each day, subtract the clock-in time from the clock-out time, then subtract unpaid breaks. Add the days together for the week. For example, 8:30 AM to 5:15 PM is 8 hours 45 minutes; minus a 30-minute lunch gives 8 hours 15 minutes, or 8.25 decimal hours.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes is 0.5, 45 minutes is 0.75. So 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 decimal hours. Payroll uses decimal hours because you can multiply them by your hourly rate.

Are lunch breaks paid?

Under federal law, meal breaks of 30 minutes or more where you are fully relieved of duty can be unpaid. Short rest breaks of about 5 to 20 minutes count as work time and must be paid. Some states have their own meal and rest break rules.

How do I calculate an overnight shift?

If your clock-out time is earlier than your clock-in time, the shift crossed midnight. Add 24 hours to the clock-out time before subtracting. This calculator does that for you, so 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM with a 30-minute break is 8 hours.

Is time card rounding legal?

Federal rules allow rounding to the nearest 5 minutes, tenth of an hour (6 minutes) or quarter hour (15 minutes), as long as it is neutral and does not always favor the employer over time. California courts are stricter, so many employers there pay exact minutes.

How is overtime calculated on a bi-weekly time card?

Overtime is counted for each workweek on its own, even if you are paid every two weeks. 50 hours in week one and 30 in week two gives 10 overtime hours, not zero. This calculator splits bi-weekly cards into two weeks for that reason.
