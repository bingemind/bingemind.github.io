What does an hourly to salary calculator do?

An hourly to salary calculator turns an hourly rate into the pay you get for a day, a week, a month and a year. It helps you compare an hourly job with a salaried job, plan a budget, or check a freelance quote. This calculator uses your real hours and the weeks you actually work, and it shows the math.

How to use the hourly to salary calculator

  1. Type your hourly rate in dollars.
  2. Type the hours you work in a normal week.
  3. Pick the weeks you work in a year. Use 50 or 48 if you take 2 or 4 unpaid weeks off.
  4. Read your pay for each period. Change any number and the results change at once.
  5. To go the other way, switch to Salary to hourly and type a yearly salary.
  6. Press Copy summary to paste the numbers into a message or a note.

The formula

Yearly pay is your hourly rate times the hours you work each week, times the weeks you work each year.

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year

A full-time year is often counted as 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 hours. At $20 an hour, that is $20 × 2,080 = $41,600 a year.

Why monthly pay is not weekly pay times 4

A year has 52 weeks but only 12 months, so an average month has about 4.33 weeks. If you multiply weekly pay by 4, you miss about one month of pay each year. That is why this calculator divides yearly pay by 12. At $800 a week, monthly pay is $41,600 ÷ 12 = $3,466.67, not $3,200.

Every two weeks or twice a month?

These sound alike but are different.

  • Every two weeks (biweekly) means 26 paydays a year. Each one is two weeks of pay.
  • Twice a month (semimonthly) means 24 paydays a year, often on the 15th and the last day of the month. Each one is yearly pay divided by 24.

At $41,600 a year, every two weeks is $1,600 and twice a month is $1,733.33.

Unpaid time off and freelance work

If you are paid only for the hours you work, weeks off cut your yearly pay. Freelancers and contractors often take 2 to 4 unpaid weeks a year for holidays, sick days and gaps between projects. Use the 50 or 48 week buttons to see the effect. At $20 an hour and 40 hours a week, 2 unpaid weeks lower yearly pay from $41,600 to $40,000.

If you have paid vacation, keep 52 weeks, because you are paid for those weeks.

Before tax

Every number on this page is gross pay, before tax and before any deductions. Your take-home pay will be lower. This calculator does not estimate tax.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly rate to a yearly salary?

Multiply your hourly rate by the hours you work each week, then by the weeks you work each year. At $20 an hour, 40 hours a week and 52 weeks, that is $41,600 a year.

$20 an hour is how much a year?

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $20 an hour is $41,600 a year before tax. That is $800 a week and about $3,466.67 a month. With 2 unpaid weeks off, it is $40,000.

How much is 40 hours a week at $25 an hour?

$25 × 40 hours is $1,000 a week. Over 52 weeks that is $52,000 a year, or about $4,333.33 a month.

How do I turn a salary into an hourly rate?

Divide the yearly salary by the hours you work in a year. For a $50,000 salary at 40 hours a week and 52 weeks, that is $50,000 ÷ 2,080 = about $24.04 an hour. Use Salary to hourly mode.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. All numbers are before tax and before deductions. Your take-home pay will be lower.

Why is monthly pay not weekly pay times 4?

A month is a little more than 4 weeks. The calculator divides yearly pay by 12, which gives the true average month.

Does it handle overtime?

Not yet. If your hours change from week to week, type your average hours per week.
