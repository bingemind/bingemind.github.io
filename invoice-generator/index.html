How to make and send an invoice

  1. Add your logo and details: upload a logo if you have one, then your business name, address and contact. Add your tax number (VAT, GST, ABN or EIN) on a line here if you are registered.
  2. Add the client: name, company and address.
  3. Check the number and dates. Use a unique, sequential invoice number. "Start next invoice" adds one for you and keeps your business details.
  4. List each item with quantity and unit price. Amounts and the subtotal are calculated for you.
  5. Tax: the standard VAT or GST rate for your country is filled in. Change it to 0 if you are not registered, or to a reduced rate if one applies. In the US, enter the sales tax rate where the customer is, if you have to collect it.
  6. Save as PDF: press "Save as PDF / Print" and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. Only the invoice is printed.
Line amount = quantity × unit price
Tax = (subtotal − discount) × tax rate
Total = subtotal − discount + tax + shipping

What an invoice must include

Rules differ by country, but a valid invoice almost always shows:

  • The word "Invoice" and a unique, sequential invoice number
  • Your name or business name, address and contact details
  • The customer's name and address
  • The invoice date and, for many tax systems, the date of supply
  • A description, quantity and price for each item
  • The total, and the tax charged with the rate
  • Payment terms and how to pay

Getting paid on time

  • Set clear terms. "Due within 14 days" or "Net 30" with an actual due date works better than "upon receipt".
  • Make paying easy. Put full bank details or a payment link on the invoice.
  • Invoice promptly, as soon as the work is delivered.
  • Know your rights on late payment. In the UK, businesses can claim statutory interest of 8% above the Bank of England base rate plus a fixed fee. EU rules set a 30 to 60 day limit for business-to-business payment. Check the rules where you trade.
  • Keep copies. Most tax authorities expect you to keep invoices for 5 to 7 years. Save each PDF and the CSV in a folder.

Invoice questions

How do I make an invoice for free?

Fill in your details, the client and each item above, then press Save as PDF / Print and choose Save as PDF. There is no sign-up, watermark or limit.

What should an invoice number look like?

Any unique, sequential format works, such as INV-0001, 2026-001 or a client prefix like ACME-012. Do not skip or reuse numbers.

Do I need to charge VAT or GST?

Only if you are registered, which is usually required once your turnover passes a threshold, for example 90,000 pounds in the UK, 75,000 dollars in Australia or 30,000 dollars in Canada. If you are not registered, set the tax rate to 0.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Press Save as PDF / Print. In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF on Windows) as the destination. Only the invoice prints, without the page around it.

Can I use a different currency?

Yes. Pick the country in the page header and the currency, number format and default tax rate change to match. 58 countries and currencies are supported.

What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?

An invoice asks for payment. A receipt confirms payment has been received. Tick Mark as paid to add a PAID stamp, then save it again as your receipt.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP file. It is resized in your browser, shown at the top of the invoice and kept for your next invoice. It is never uploaded.

Is my information stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so the form is filled in when you come back. Nothing is sent to a server.
