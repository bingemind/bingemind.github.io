About

Press Release Formatter puts the parts of a press release in the standard order: release line, media contact, headline, dateline, body, About section and end mark. You write the text. It formats the date, checks for missing parts and gives you plain text or a PDF.

What it does

Most press release templates are files you have to download and fix by hand. This page does the layout as you type, so you can focus on the news.

How it works

The tool runs in your browser. We do not ask you to create an account. It does not write or change your words. It only orders, formats and checks them.
