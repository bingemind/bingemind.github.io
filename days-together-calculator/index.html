How many days have we been together?

This days together calculator counts the days between your start date and today. Type the date you met, the day you made it official or your wedding day. The count shows at once. You also see the same time in weeks, in months, and in years, months and days.

The tool counts calendar days. A clock change in spring or autumn does not add or take away a day. By default the start date is day 0, so the day after it is "1 day together". Many couples count the first day as day 1 instead. Turn on "Count the start date as day 1" under More options and every number and date moves to match.

How to count your days together

  1. Pick the start date. That can be your first date, your wedding day, the day you got sober or the day your pet came home.
  2. Choose what you are counting and add your names if you like.
  3. Read the count. You see days, weeks, months and years, and the date of your next anniversary.
  4. Look down the milestone list. Press "Make card" on the day you want to mark.
  5. Pick a look, colors and a size. Change any words on the card.
  6. Download a PNG for your phone, or a PDF to print. Print at 100% and cut on the marks.

Milestones worth marking

Day 100 comes a little over three months in. Day 365 is one full year, or day 366 if a February 29 falls in between. Day 500 lands about one year and four months in. Day 1,000 is about two years and nine months. Day 10,000 is about 27 years and four months. The list shows the exact date and the weekday for each one, so you can plan a dinner or a small surprise. Add your own number, such as day 1,234, and it joins the list.

Anniversaries on February 29

If you started on February 29, your anniversary falls on February 28 in years that have no February 29. In leap years it falls on February 29 again. The calculator follows this rule for the next anniversary line.

Make a milestone card

Press "Make card" next to any milestone, or keep today's count. Choose Classic, Bold or Minimal, then a color set. The 5 x 7 and 4 x 6 inch cards print at their true size in the middle of a Letter or A4 sheet, with small marks to show where to cut. Print at 100% or "Actual size". The square and story sizes are for a post or a story on your phone. Every line on the card can change, so you can write your own message.

Not just for couples

Count sober days, days since a pet came home, days of a friendship, or a baby's age in days. Pick the matching option and the card words change to fit. You can still edit every word.

Your dates stay on your device

The count and the card are made in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server. The tool saves your dates and words on this device so a reload does not lose them. Press "Start over" to clear them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count how many days we have been together?

Type your start date in the box at the top. The count shows at once in days, weeks, months and years. It counts up to today unless you pick another date under More options.

Does the count include the first day?

By default the start date is day 0, so the next day is day 1. Turn on "Count the start date as day 1" under More options if you want the first day to count.

When is our 1,000th day?

Look for Day 1,000 in the milestone list. It shows the date, the weekday and how many days are left. Day 1,000 is about two years and nine months after the start.

What happens if we started on February 29?

Your anniversary falls on February 28 in years without a February 29, and on February 29 in leap years.

Can I print the milestone card?

Yes. Choose the 5 x 7 or 4 x 6 inch card and press Download PDF or Print. The card sits at true size on a Letter or A4 sheet with cut marks. Print at 100% or "Actual size".

Is it free, and is my date saved anywhere?

It is free with no sign-up and no watermark. Your date stays in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Press Start over to remove it.

Can I count sober days or a pet's days at home?

Yes. Choose Sober, Pet, Baby or Friends in "What are you counting?". The card words change to match, and you can edit them.
