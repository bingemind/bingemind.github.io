A newspaper clipping generator with your photo

This tool makes one old newspaper story, cut out of the page. You write the newspaper name, the date line, the headline and the story. You can add a photo and a caption. The tool draws it all on yellowed paper with torn edges, a fold and old type. Then you download a PNG or a PDF.

Your photo stays on your device. The page reads it with your browser and draws it on the clipping. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. The download has no watermark.

How to make an old newspaper clipping

  1. Pick a starter text. Family scrapbook, birthday, mystery party, fantasy town or school project. Or pick Blank and write your own.
  2. Change the words. Type the newspaper name, the date line, the headline and the story. The preview changes as you type.
  3. Add a photo. Tap Add photo. The photo is printed in black and white, like a real old paper. Use Zoom and the two sliders to crop it.
  4. Pick a look. Choose the 1890s, the 1920s or the 1950s. Choose torn or straight edges, how old it looks, a fold line and a coffee stain.
  5. Download. Download PNG gives you the clipping with a clear background. Download PDF puts it on a Letter or A4 page, ready to print.

Ideas for your clipping

  • Family scrapbook: the day a family shop opened, or how grandparents met. Add an old photo for the best effect.
  • Birthday card: a short story about the birthday person. Print it and glue it inside a card.
  • Mystery party clue: a strange story from a made-up town. Hand it to guests as the first clue.
  • Tabletop game handout: news from a fantasy town for your players. The 1890s look fits an old world best.
  • School project: a history lesson or a book report written as a news story.
  • Wedding or anniversary: the story of the couple, as if it was in the paper the day they met.

A clear PNG for slides and scrapbooks

The PNG has a clear background outside the torn edge. You can put it on a slide, a poster or a digital scrapbook page, and the torn edge shows over your own background. Choose White under PNG background if you want a plain white file instead.

Print it at the right size

The PNG is 1500 pixels wide at 300 dots per inch. That prints 5 inches wide, about the size of a real clipping. The height follows the story, from about 3 to 7.5 inches. The PDF puts the clipping in the middle of a Letter or A4 page at the same size. In the print dialog, choose "Actual size" or 100%. Print it on cream or brown paper to make it look even older.

Tips for an old look

  • Write the headline in the present tense and keep it short: "Dragon Seen Near the Old Bridge".
  • Keep the story to two short paragraphs. Old clippings are small, and a long story is cut at the end.
  • Use an old style date line, such as "Saturday, May 3, 1924".
  • After you print, you can crumple the paper and flatten it again for real creases.

Keep it a novelty

A clipping you make here is a craft, a prop or a gift. Each clipping has a small "Novelty print. Fictional." line at the bottom. You can turn it off under Look. The PNG file keeps this note in its details either way. Please do not use the tool to fool people or to say false things about a real person. The starter texts use made-up towns and newspaper names, and the looks are our own designs. They do not copy any real newspaper.

Frequently asked questions

Is this newspaper clipping generator free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads. You can make and print as many clippings as you like.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your browser reads the photo and draws it on the clipping on your own device. The photo is not sent to a server and it is not saved. You can check this in the Network tab of your browser tools.

Can I get the clipping with a clear background?

Yes. Download PNG gives you a file with a clear background outside the torn edge, so it sits well on a slide or a scrapbook page. Choose White under PNG background for a plain white file.

How big is the clipping when I print it?

The PNG is 1500 pixels wide at 300 dots per inch, which prints 5 inches wide. The height follows the story, from about 3 to 7.5 inches. The PDF puts it in the middle of a Letter or A4 page at that size. Print at Actual size or 100%.

Can I use it for a mystery party or a tabletop game?

Yes. Pick the Mystery party or Fantasy town starter text, then change the words to fit your story. The 1890s look with torn edges works well as a handout.

Can I add a photo of my family?

Yes. Tap Add photo and pick a picture. It is printed in black and white or as halftone dots, like an old paper. Use Zoom and the sliders to crop it.
