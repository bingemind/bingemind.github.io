    Scavenger hunt clues that fit your home

    Printed clue sets are written for someone else's house. This tool makes scavenger hunt clue cards for the spots you really have. Pick the sofa, the fridge door, the shoes by the door or the swing in the yard. You get a clue for each spot, numbered cards to print, and an answer key that tells you where each card goes.

    There are 40 spots to choose from, and you can add your own. Each spot has a rhyming clue for kids and a riddle for adults.

    How to set up a scavenger hunt

    1. Choose Kids or Adults, and pick a theme.
    2. Pick your hiding spots in the order you want, or press "Pick for me".
    3. Choose where the prize will wait.
    4. Read the clues and change any you like.
    5. Print the cards. Cut on the dashed lines.
    6. Use the answer key: hide each card where it says, and put the prize and the prize note in the last spot.
    7. Hand the player the start card.

    How the clues connect

    The player gets the start card first. Its clue leads to the first hiding spot. The card hidden there leads to the next spot, and so on. The last card leads to the prize. So each card is hidden at the spot the card before it describes. The answer key lists this for you, card by card. Each card can also show where it is hidden in small grey text, which helps you set up without a list in your hand. The spot it names is the one the player has already found, so it gives nothing away.

    Clues for kids and for adults

    Kid clues are short rhymes with simple words, like "Where do we sit to watch a show? Lift a soft square and look below." Read them aloud to children who cannot read yet. Adult clues are riddles that take a little more thought, good for a partner, teens or a party. You can change the words of any clue before you print.

    How many clues to use

    For children under 6, use 3 to 5 clues. For ages 6 to 10, 6 to 10 clues work well. Teens and adults enjoy 8 to 15. Plan a few minutes per clue, and more for young children. Use 4 cards per sheet for bigger text, or 6 per sheet to save paper.

    Themes for every holiday

    Choose Birthday, Easter, Winter holidays, Halloween, Date night or Plain. The theme changes the start card, the prize note and the colors. For an Easter egg hunt, hide a small egg with each card. For a birthday, put the gift at the prize spot.

    Safe hiding spots

    The list leaves out stairs, ovens, stoves, medicine cabinets, pools and anything near a road. If you add your own spots, keep them safe and easy to reach. Hide cards at a child's height for young players.

    Private and free

    The tool runs in your browser. The names and clues you type stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no sign-up.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do scavenger hunt clues work?

    The player gets the start card. Its clue leads to the first hiding spot, where the next card waits. Each card leads to the next spot until the last one leads to the prize.

    Where do I hide each card?

    Use the answer key on the last page. It lists every card and the spot to hide it. Each card can also show its spot in small grey text.

    How many clues should a scavenger hunt have?

    Use 3 to 5 clues for children under 6, 6 to 10 for ages 6 to 10, and 8 to 15 for teens and adults.

    Can I write my own clues?

    Yes. Change the words of any clue, or add your own hiding spot with your own clue.

    Are there clues for adults?

    Yes. Choose Adults to get riddles instead of rhymes. The Date night theme fits an anniversary or a gift hunt.

    What paper size do the cards use?

    Letter or A4, with 4 or 6 cards per sheet and dashed lines to cut on. Print at 100% or "Actual size".

    Is it free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit, and nothing you type is uploaded.
