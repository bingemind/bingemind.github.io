    Make it festive

    • Print on white or kraft paper and use Filled letters in red or green.
    • Use Outline and let children color the letters with markers or glitter glue.
    • Hang the banner over a fireplace with two small hooks and a length of twine.
    • Add small clips or tape to hold the pages flat on the string.

    Printing

    Set the print dialog to 100% or Actual size. Do not choose Fit to page, because that shrinks the pennant and moves the punch marks.

    Print at actual size

    In the print dialog, choose 100% or Actual size. Do not choose Fit to page. That setting shrinks the page, and the punch marks move.

    Questions

    How many pages is Merry Christmas?

    MERRY CHRISTMAS has 14 letters, so it uses 14 pages. A blank page between the words adds one more.

    Can I add a year or a name?

    Yes. Type it after the message. Numbers and many symbols work.

    Which colors are available?

    Black, red, orange, green, blue and purple, plus a rainbow option that changes the color on every page.
