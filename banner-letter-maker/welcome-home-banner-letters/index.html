    How to hang it

    • Print all pages and cut out the shapes along the dashed line.
    • Punch two holes at the top of each page, where the marks show.
    • Thread a long string through the holes in order. Page numbers help if you turn them on.
    • Hang the string across a doorway, a mantel or a porch.

    Which style looks best

    A pennant in one solid color looks neat from a distance. A rectangle with a thin border looks like a sign and works well indoors. Use landscape pages if the banner is long and you want it to look wide.

    Print at actual size

    In the print dialog, choose 100% or Actual size. Do not choose Fit to page. That setting shrinks the page, and the punch marks move.

    Questions

    How many pages does Welcome Home need?

    WELCOME has 7 letters and HOME has 4, so the banner uses 11 pages. A blank spacer page adds one more.

    Can I print it in black and white?

    Yes. Pick Black and Outline. It uses very little ink, and you can print on colored paper.
