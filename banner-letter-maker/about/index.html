About

Banner Letter Maker turns a short message into a banner you can print at home. Each page holds one letter, number or symbol, as large as the paper allows. You print the pages, cut them out and hang them on string.

Who it is for

Parents planning a birthday, teachers decorating a classroom, hosts of baby showers and welcome home parties, and office teams that want a quick sign. It works on a phone or a computer.

How to use it

Type your message. Pick Letter or A4 paper, portrait or landscape, a shape, a font and a color. Choose Outline to save ink or Filled for solid letters. Then press Download PDF and print at 100% or Actual size.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. The letters are drawn from font outlines stored on this site, and the PDF is built on your device. Your message is not sent to a server, and we do not ask you to create an account. The tool saves your settings, such as the paper size, on your own device. It does not save your message.

The four fonts are Anton, Lilita One, Alfa Slab One and Titan One. All four are free to use under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
