How to add and remove sales tax or VAT

Add tax: total = price × (1 + rate ÷ 100)
Remove tax: price before tax = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)
Tax amount = total − price before tax

Adding tax. A 100.00 item with 7.25% tax costs 100 × 1.0725 = 107.25.

Removing tax. A 120.00 total that includes 20% VAT has a net price of 120 ÷ 1.20 = 100.00, and 20.00 of VAT.

A common mistake when removing tax is to take the percentage off the total. 20% of 120 is 24, not 20. Always divide by 1 plus the rate.

Sales tax in the United States

There is no national sales tax. Each state sets its own rate, and cities, counties and special districts add theirs. This calculator fills in the state rate, and you add the local rate.

  • No state sales tax: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon. Some Alaskan towns charge a local tax.
  • Highest state rates: California at 7.25%, then Indiana, Mississippi, Rhode Island and Tennessee at 7%.
  • Highest combined rates: parts of Louisiana, Tennessee, Arkansas, Washington and Alabama exceed 9% to 10% once local taxes are added.
  • Exemptions: many states do not tax groceries or prescription medicines, and several hold sales tax holidays.

Prices in US shops are shown before tax, so the till total is higher than the shelf price.

For businesses: invoices and reverse calculations

  • Pricing: decide whether your prices are quoted net (business customers) or gross (consumers), and say which on quotes.
  • Working back from a receipt: use "Tax included" to split a total into net and tax for your books.
  • Registration thresholds: small businesses below a turnover limit often do not have to register, for example 90,000 pounds in the UK.
  • Cross-border sales: in the EU, sales to consumers in other member states are taxed at the customer's country rate once you pass the 10,000 euro threshold. US sellers can owe sales tax in states where they pass an economic nexus threshold.
  • Canada: HST provinces charge one combined rate. British Columbia, Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Quebec charge GST plus a separate provincial tax.

Sales tax and VAT questions

How do I calculate sales tax?

Multiply the price by the tax rate as a decimal. For a 50.00 item at 8% tax, the tax is 50 × 0.08 = 4.00 and the total is 54.00.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the total by 1 plus the VAT rate. With 20% VAT, divide by 1.2. A 60.00 total is 50.00 before VAT with 10.00 of VAT.

How do I work out the sales tax from a total?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate to get the pre-tax price, then subtract that from the total. For 107.25 at 7.25%, the price is 100.00 and the tax is 7.25.

Which US states have no sales tax?

Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no state sales tax. Local taxes can still apply in parts of Alaska.

What is the difference between sales tax and VAT?

Sales tax is charged once, at the final sale to the customer. VAT is charged at each stage of production, and businesses reclaim the VAT they paid, so the final consumer bears it. VAT is normally included in the displayed price.

What is the GST rate in India in 2026?

After the September 2025 reform, most goods and services fall into a 5% or 18% slab, with 40% on certain luxury and sin goods and 0% on essentials.

What is HST in Canada?

Harmonized Sales Tax combines the 5% federal GST with the provincial sales tax into one rate: 13% in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia and 15% in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Prince Edward Island.
