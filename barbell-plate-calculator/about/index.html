About

Barbell Plate Calculator shows which plates to load on each side of a barbell for the weight you want to lift. It works in pounds and kilograms, with any bar and any set of plates.

What it does

It gives the answer as you type, shows the loaded bar, and only uses the plate sizes you turn on.

How it works

The tool subtracts the bar (and collars, if you turn them on) from your target weight, halves the rest, and finds the fewest plates that make that weight on one side. If the exact weight is not possible with your plates, it shows the closest load and a one-tap button for the next load on the other side.

The calculator runs in your browser and does not send what you type. We do not ask you to create an account. Your settings stay on your device.

A note on safety

This tool helps with arithmetic. It does not replace a coach. Always check that plates sit fully on the sleeve, use collars when you lift, and ask for a spotter on heavy sets.
