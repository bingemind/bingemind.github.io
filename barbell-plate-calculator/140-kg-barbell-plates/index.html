How to load 140 kg

A 140 kg barbell is the 20 kg bar plus 60 kg on each side. It is a serious working weight for the squat and the deadlift.

  1. Subtract the bar. 140 kg minus 20 kg is 120 kg.
  2. Divide by 2. Each side carries 60 kg.
  3. Load two 25 kg plates and one 10 kg plate on each side. Put the heaviest plate against the shoulder of the bar.
  4. Add a collar to each sleeve and check that both sides look the same.

A 140 kg squat or deadlift is a common intermediate target. It is also a typical opener for a lifter in a first powerlifting meet.

A tip for 140 kg

Use the 1.25 kg plates only for the last small jump. At 140 kg a 2.5 kg change in the total is under 2 percent, so you can skip them on a normal day.

Questions about 140 kg

How many plates do I need for 140 kg?

With a 20 kg bar you need 60 kg on each side: two 25 kg plates and one 10 kg plate. That is 6 plates on the bar.

How much is 140 kg in lb?

140 kg is 308.6 lb. If your gym uses lb plates, switch the unit in the calculator and it picks a nearby load for you.
