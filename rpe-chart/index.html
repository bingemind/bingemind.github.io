Where these numbers come from

Current RTS (default). The current Reactive Training Systems (RTS) table, the one in their TRAC system. We read it from the open data file data-raw/rpe_tab_rts.csv in the R package strength (github.com/alexpaynter/strength). Its documentation says the table was pulled from TRAC in December 2025. Reps 1 to 12 and RPE 6 to 10 are shown.

2016 RTS article. The chart from the RTS article "Customizing Your RPE Chart" (January 6, 2016). We read it as copied in the open-source RPECalc app (github.com/tnapes96/RPECalc). That table stops at RPE 6.5, so we add the RPE 6 row with the reps plus RIR rule: reps and reps in reserve (RIR) add up to the same percentage.

The two versions are not the same. They differ by up to 7.6 points at high reps (12 reps at RPE 6 is 65.0% in the current table and 57.4% in the 2016 chart), and they agree within 0.1 point whenever reps plus reps in reserve is 10 or less (for example 6 reps at RPE 6, or 10 reps at RPE 10). Use the version your coach uses, or edit any value. The RPE scale based on reps in reserve comes from Zourdos et al., 2016, and Helms et al., 2016.

What an RPE chart shows

RPE means rate of perceived exertion. In lifting, it is a score from 1 to 10 for how hard a set felt. An RPE of 10 is a set with nothing left. An RPE of 8 is a set where you could have done about 2 more reps. RIR means reps in reserve. It is the same idea, counted the other way: RPE 8 equals 2 RIR.

An RPE chart turns that score into a share of your one-rep max (1RM). Find your reps and your RPE, and the chart gives a percentage. Many lifters use it to pick the weight for the day. On a strong day the bar moves fast, so they go up. On a bad day they stay put and still hit the planned RPE.

How to use the RPE calculator

  1. Pick pounds or kilograms, and pick RPE or RIR.
  2. Enter the weight of a hard set you did, the reps you did, and how hard it felt.
  3. Read your estimated 1RM. It is the weight divided by the chart percentage.
  4. Under "Plan your next set", pick the reps and RPE you want. The tool shows the load for that set.
  5. Choose the smallest jump your plates can make. The load rounds to it.

A worked example

You squat 225 lb for 5 reps, and it felt like RPE 8. The chart shows 81.1 percent for 5 reps at RPE 8. Your estimated 1RM is 225 ÷ 0.811 = 277.4 lb.

Next you want 3 reps at RPE 8. The chart shows 86.3 percent. That is 277.4 × 0.863 = 239.4 lb. With 5 lb jumps, you load 240 lb.

Where the numbers come from

Current RTS (default). The current RTS table from their TRAC system. We read it from the open data file rpe_tab_rts.csv in the R package strength. Its documentation says the table was pulled from TRAC in December 2025. We did not read it on the RTS site itself.

2016 RTS article. The chart from the RTS article "Customizing Your RPE Chart" (January 6, 2016), as copied in the open-source RPECalc app. That table ends at RPE 6.5. We add the RPE 6 row with the reps plus RIR rule.

Both charts follow one rule: reps plus reps in reserve decides the percentage. Five reps at RPE 8 leave 2 in reserve, so they use the same percentage as 7 reps at RPE 10. The RPE 6 values of the 2016 version are extended this way, because that article has no RPE 6 row.

The RPE scale based on reps in reserve comes from Zourdos and colleagues (2016, Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research), who tested it on the squat. Helms and colleagues (2016, Strength and Conditioning Journal) explained how to apply it in training.

Limits you should know

The chart gives an estimate, not a test. People rate effort in different ways. New lifters often rate a set as easier or harder than it was. Ratings tend to be more accurate on heavy sets close to failure, and they improve with practice. Above about 10 reps, expect a rough estimate: it is harder to judge how many reps are left in a long set, and the two chart versions differ most there. Edit any value to match the chart your coach uses.

Frequently asked questions

What does RPE mean in lifting?

RPE means rate of perceived exertion. It is a score from 1 to 10 for how hard a set felt. RPE 10 means you had no reps left. RPE 8 means you could have done about 2 more reps.

What is the difference between RPE and RIR?

They describe the same thing in two ways. RIR is reps in reserve, the number of reps you had left. RPE is 10 minus RIR. So 2 RIR is RPE 8, and 0 RIR is RPE 10. Use the RIR switch if you think in reps left.

How do I convert RPE to a percentage of 1RM?

Find your reps and your RPE on the chart. The cell shows the share of your 1RM. For example, 5 reps at RPE 8 is 81.1 percent. Divide your weight by 0.811 to estimate your 1RM.

How do I find the weight for 3 reps at RPE 8?

Enter a recent hard set to get your estimated 1RM. Then pick 3 reps and RPE 8 under "Plan your next set". The chart gives 86.3 percent, and the tool multiplies it by your 1RM and rounds to your plates.

Which RPE chart does this use?

It has two versions of the chart from Mike Tuchscherer of Reactive Training Systems (RTS). The default is the current RTS table (TRAC), read from the R package strength. The other is the chart from the 2016 RTS article "Customizing Your RPE Chart". They differ by up to 7.6 points at high reps, for example 12 reps at RPE 6 is 65.0 percent in one and 57.4 in the other. Pick the version your coach uses, or edit any value.

How accurate is an RPE-based 1RM?

It is an estimate. It depends on how well you judge your effort. Ratings tend to be better on heavy sets close to failure and for lifters with more practice. Estimates tend to be most reliable for heavy sets of 1 to 5 reps close to failure. Above about 10 reps they are rough, and the two chart versions differ most there.

Why does the weight round up or down?

The tool rounds to the smallest jump your plates can make. That is two of your smallest plates, one on each side. Pick 1.25, 2.5, 5 or 10 lb, or 0.5, 1, 2.5 or 5 kg, under "Round to the nearest".

Can I change the chart values?

Yes. Click any number in the chart and type your own. The calculator uses your value at once. Your changes stay on your device and belong to the chart version you picked. One button resets them.
