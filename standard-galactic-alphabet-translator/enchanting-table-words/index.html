What does the enchanting table say?

The glyphs stand for ordinary letters. Reading them gives words such as light, darkness, fire and air. Some entries are playful references, such as klaatu, berata and niktu. Others are short words that help the table make a phrase.

The list has 62 entries for Java Edition. Seven entries are Java only: phnglui, mglwnafh, cthulhu, rlyeh, wgahnagl, fhtagn and baguette. The remaining 55 form the shared list. The random example above draws three to five words from the full list. It is a reading exercise, not a game enchantment simulator.

Can these words help me choose an enchantment?

No. The writing is cosmetic. A phrase that contains fire does not promise a fire enchantment. Translating the words is useful for learning the alphabet and solving a screenshot puzzle, but it does not reveal the result of enchanting an item.

Read a phrase yourself

Start at the left and replace each glyph with its English letter. Keep spaces between words. You can compare the English word under each entry with its symbols here, or hide the screen below the glyphs and test your memory.

To decode a screenshot, open the Standard Galactic Alphabet translator and tap its glyph keys. For a paper reference, use the printable alphabet key.
