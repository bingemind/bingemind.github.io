Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Your text stays on your device

The translator, image maker and worksheets run in your browser. We do not upload your text, names or images. The site does not have accounts, contact forms, comments, analytics, ads or social widgets.

Share links

The Share link button puts your text after the # in the address. This part is not sent to the web server. Anyone you give the link to can read the text. Do not share private messages.

Local storage

Only your light or dark theme choice is saved in this browser. Clear site data in your browser to remove it.

Hosting and children

Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages. Children may use this tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can contact us if they have a concern.
