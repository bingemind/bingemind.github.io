About

DPI Changer changes the DPI value of JPEG and PNG images. It is free, and it needs no account.

What it is for

Print shops and Etsy listings often ask for 300 DPI files. This tool does only the DPI change, and it does it in your browser.

How it works

A JPEG or PNG file stores its DPI in a small header. The tool rewrites those few bytes and leaves the pixel data as it is. Nothing is decoded or compressed again, so the quality does not change.

All of it runs in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
